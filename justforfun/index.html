Aviation Project

upwind

Concept · Brand · Design · Web

A live map of Michigan’s general aviation: every small plane in the air and the conditions at every airport, all happening now. It exists to show how much flying really happens here, and to make the case for the small fields that keep it going. The idea and the design all come from me, from the name and the livery to every screen.

Hospitality Concept

Amíra

Concept · Brand · Design · Web

My own concept for a luxury micro resort on Northern Michigan’s Lake Michigan shore. The idea and the design all come from me: the name, the brand, the residences and the website. Amíra takes its name from ami, to love, and mira, to look. To love a place is to really see it.

Audience growth

Four ship launches: three as a designer, one as Creative Manager. By Brilliant Lady we’d stopped telling people about a launch and started bringing them along for it, from the first countdown to maiden-voyage moments in New York City. Between launches, the posts below kept that growth going: I jumped on trends, tested new formats and styles, and pushed to lead the cruise industry on social, not follow it.

Brand standards

One source of truth

The Social Design System

Social has its own look within the wider Virgin Voyages brand, so it needed its own guidelines: one place that answers how every format and style of creative should look. I designed it first in Figma from our existing work, then, knowing our team would want something more engaging, used Claude to assemble that same content into an interactive digital book. It evolves with the team and gets new hires and contractors on brand quickly.
